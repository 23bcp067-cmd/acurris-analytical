## The problem

Milk enters your supply from hundreds or thousands of collection points, and one contaminated can spoils a pooled tanker. Testing has to happen at the point of collection, every day, by staff who are not lab analysts.

## What we supply

- **Antibiotic residue tests.** Beta-lactam and tetracycline tests for accept or reject decisions at collection, plus other drug classes for plant intake.
- **Adulteration panels.** Urea, starch, detergent and synthetic milk tests that read as a color change, with no instruments.
- **Mycotoxin kits.** For the feed your farmers use, upstream of aflatoxin in milk.

## How we work with dairies

We match kits to the incubators and equipment you already have. Our technical team trains your staff by video call and replies to questions within 48 hours. Run an evaluation pack at one collection center before you roll out across the network.
