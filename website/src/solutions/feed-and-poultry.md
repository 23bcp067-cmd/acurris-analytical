## The problem

Feed ingredients rarely carry just one mycotoxin. A survey of 540 feed and raw material samples from across India found more than one mycotoxin in 95 percent of them ([survey report](https://benisonmedia.com/a-comprehensive-survey-of-mycotoxin-prevalence-in-india-2017-18/)). Contaminated feed shows up later as poor feed conversion and flock health problems, long after the lot was accepted.

## What we supply

- **Rapid mycotoxin kits.** Aflatoxin B1 and total aflatoxins, ochratoxin A, DON, zearalenone and fumonisins.
- **Sampling guidance.** Most mycotoxin error comes from the sample, not the test. We help you set increment counts and grinding steps for your lot sizes.
- **Sample preparation.** SPE cartridges and consumables if you send samples on to confirmatory analysis.

## How we work with feed producers

Tell us your ingredients and daily intake volume. We recommend a screening panel and reply within 48 hours. Run an evaluation pack on your own maize or oilcake before you commit.
