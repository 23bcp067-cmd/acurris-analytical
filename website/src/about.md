## What we do

We source rapid screening kits and sample preparation consumables from established manufacturers, sell them under the Acurris Analytical name, and support them with a technical team in India. One supplier covers screening at intake and preparation for confirmatory analysis.

## Who we work with

Food testing laboratories, dairy processors and cooperatives, and feed mills and poultry integrators. We also supply exporters, food manufacturers and research labs.
