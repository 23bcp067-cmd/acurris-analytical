## The problem

Your clients send everything: grain for mycotoxins, produce for pesticides, milk for residues, spices for colors. Each analyte needs its own consumables, and a lab that buys from many overseas suppliers spends time on import paperwork and waits on stock.

India is adding food testing capacity fast. The central government targeted 100 new NABL-accredited food testing labs for 2025-26 alone ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2114057)). New labs need reliable supply from day one.

## What we supply

- **Sample preparation for chromatography.** QuEChERS salts in original, AOAC 2007.01 and EN 15662 formulations, dSPE cleanup tubes, SPE cartridges, syringe filters and vials.
- **Rapid screening kits.** For mycotoxins, antibiotic residues, adulterants, pesticides, pathogens and allergens, so you can triage samples before the instrument queue.
- **Formulation matching.** Send us the formulation and fill weights from your validated method and we confirm a match before you order, so you avoid revalidation.

## How we work with labs

One quote covers kits and consumables. A technical team member answers method questions within 48 hours. Evaluation packs let your analysts check recoveries on your own matrices before you switch.
