## What we use

This site uses no advertising cookies. It uses Google Analytics only if you accept analytics cookies in the banner. Until you accept, Google Analytics does not load.

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Counts visits and shows which pages and buttons are used | Up to 2 years |

We also store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (`aa_consent`) so we don't ask again on every page. It contains no personal data.

## Changing your choice

Use the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of any page to accept or reject analytics cookies. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
